# Storefront — Experience / Operate

## Direction contract
THESIS: An independent streetwear showroom organized around a physical clothing rail. The garments introduce the collection before the interface asks for a choice.

OWN-WORLD: Warm studio gray, almost black and vermilion. Archivo's compressed heavy lettering, Manrope's precise interface, thin rules and unboxed imagery. Product graphics provide the other colors.

STORY: See four suspended pieces, move through a changing rail, inspect the selection, explore the graphic details, and save pieces in the bag. Commercial facts stay explicitly pending.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Discreet horizontal navigation. Two enormous left-aligned lines WEAR YOUR / ROOTS. sharing the upper field with a small manifesto and round downward link. A full-width wire with four large photographic garments crosses the lower field. Black collection CTA, rail progress and descriptive labels anchor its base.

FORM: User-specified clothing-rail concept, directly implemented from the explicit brief; no randomized concept or confirmation required by the user. Signature interaction is a short sticky rail exchange with independent horizontal translation and rotation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
