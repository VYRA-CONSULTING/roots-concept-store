## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind as explicitly requested for a new project. GSAP coordinates the two scroll sequences; CSS handles UI feedback. No duplicate motion framework.

## Users and purpose
Spanish-speaking streetwear shoppers discovering graphic t-shirts through an editorial storefront, inspecting pieces and saving selections in a persistent local bag.

## Confirmed constraints
Use ROOTS Concept Store and the supplied Instagram URL. Four supplied ARKAD graphic t-shirts define the initial selection. Do not fabricate prices, stock, sizes, materials, brand history, launches, contact details, policies or completed transactions. Local data service must remain replaceable by an API. Reduced motion, keyboard navigation and responsive layouts are required.

## Evidence and open decisions
Workspace initially empty; user images visible in conversation but unavailable as original files. Derived editorial garment image explicitly documented in ASSETS.md; originals requested asynchronously. Product names are descriptive editorial labels. Prices, size options, inventory, currencies, checkout and commercial policies remain unconfigured. No payments or remote orders. Instagram could not be inspected.
